## Shared measurement rules

Use a fixed campaign population and an explicit UTC cutoff. Count unique entitlement IDs unless a metric specifies an action ID. Do not count an unverified implementation as a completed removal. Show a blank or “Not applicable” for a zero denominator, never a misleading 0% or 100%.

| KPI | Numerator | Denominator / unit | SYN-REV-001 result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision completion | Eligible entitlements with Retain, Modify or Revoke decision | All eligible entitlements | 5 / 6 = 83.3% |
| Pending decision count | Eligible entitlements without a decision | Count | 1 |
| Verified removal completion | Required removal actions independently verified by cutoff | All removal actions generated by the campaign | 2 / 3 = 66.7% |
| Overdue removal rate | Required actions past due and not verified by cutoff | All required actions due by cutoff | 1 / 3 = 33.3% |
| On-time verified removal rate | Actions verified on or before their due date | All required actions due by cutoff, including still-open actions | 2 / 3 = 66.7% |
| Expired exception count | Exceptions past expiry with access still effective | Count | Not available in this dataset |

For this example, all three removal actions are due before the cutoff. A future-due action would appear in overall remediation completion but not the overdue or on-time denominator yet.

## Operational use

- Low decision completion requires reviewer follow-up, not a claim of failed technical controls.
- Low verified removal completion requires custodian and verifier follow-up.
- An overdue privileged action warrants risk-based escalation even when the overall percentage is small.
- Report excluded entitlements and unknowns alongside rates to prevent a narrow denominator from hiding risk.

## Data-quality checks

Require a unique entitlement key, a valid decision, a decision timestamp for completed decisions, a due date for removal actions and a verification timestamp for completed removals. Reject verification before implementation. Do not infer verification from a closed service ticket.

The synthetic evidence table demonstrates calculation logic. It is not an automated control implementation or a complete production event schema.
